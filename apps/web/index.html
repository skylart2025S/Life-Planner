About saving on this device

Plans are saved only in this browser, on this device. They are not backed up or synced to an account, your wardrobe, or an external calendar. Clearing site data removes them. Each plan reserves its time; overlapping plans are rejected. Dates use your browser’s timezone. Ambiguous or missing daylight-saving times must be changed before saving.

Reloading discards unsaved form changes after confirmation.
